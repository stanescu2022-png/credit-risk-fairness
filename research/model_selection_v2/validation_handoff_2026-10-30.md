# Independent-validation handoff

Specified **30 October 2026**, before selection-exploration results and new
historical-holdout predictions. This is a proposed next-stage design, not an
executed confirmation study or a substitute for the fixed exploratory judge v1.

## What the present study can decide

The fixed pilot can establish whether the specified outcome screen changes
SAFE's choice; which declared risks account for a change; whether joint scenarios
change the decision beyond marginal scenarios; and the resulting exploratory
out-of-sample trade-offs. The synthetic audit establishes a scoped measurement
limitation. Neither establishes novelty, population superiority or publishability.

Interpret the complete baseline/ablation panel, not only the thesis contrast:

- If the extension and SAFE select the same model, their predictions coincide.
  Additional diagnostics do not create an empirical selection improvement.
- If the extension matches a simpler clean-loss or worst-loss selector, this
  pool does not establish a distinct benefit from combining it with SAFE.
- If the full and marginal screens coincide, there is no observed point-choice
  contribution from the joint axes in this pool. Conditional bootstrap changes
  can describe sensitivity, but cannot be relabelled as independent improvement.
- A nonzero factorial loss contrast is not a causal or nonlinear-model claim.
  Selection relevance requires examining candidate comparisons and decisions.
- Removing a dominated original choice does not guarantee that the chosen
  frontier replacement dominates it. Inspect the actual complete judge vector.
- No practical equivalence follows from an interval containing zero. No joint
  dominance certificate follows from separate marginal percentile intervals.

Negative and inconclusive findings remain part of the study. They may support
a narrower measurement-scope paper or motivate a separately versioned hypothesis;
they do not justify retroactively changing the v1 judge or deleting baselines.

## Requirements before a stronger empirical claim

Choose the claim before the new evaluation outcomes are accessed. Distinguish a
Pareto trade-off, a benefit on a named risk, and all-coordinate superiority. The
current primary evaluation remains null; a later primary outcome or meaningful
margin needs substantive justification and prospective documentation.

Select an additional dataset on documented access and relevance criteria:
binary credit-default meaning and prediction timing, permitted use, predictors
available at the decision time, available disparity metadata, and enough support
for the declared rates. Record considered datasets and reasons for exclusion
without screening them for favorable method results. Romanian data are not
available in the present workspace. No particular new dataset is selected here.

Scenario construction must be specified from the data dictionary and domain
rationale before evaluation outcomes. The current three six-variable families
and 22-predictor transforms are Taiwan-specific; they cannot silently become
universal feed definitions. An external feature-family mapping, missingness
operation or fault design needs explicit documentation. Equal synthetic exposure
does not establish equal real fault frequency or plausible production damage.

Freeze the complete procedure: candidate/preprocessing definitions and shared
development resources; group/time separation appropriate to the new data;
fitting, selection and evaluation roles; all baselines/ablations; reference and
sensitivity scenarios; selector tie rules; judge axes; uncertainty and missing-
rate handling; and the claim's failure conditions. Freeze before new evaluation
predictions. Refitting and tuning require that separately specified version;
the current frozen pilot is not refitted as part of inspection.

Assess fitting and selection variability by repeating the entire procedure under
a prespecified design. Each replicate must fit preprocessing on its fitting role,
select using only its selection role, and evaluate frozen choices separately.
Preserve whole-original-predictor groups. Overlapping splits are not independent
replicates; report their dependence and do not use a naive independent-samples
test. Fixed-prediction bootstrap uncertainty in the current study does not cover
this variability. Specify the number of datasets/replicates and analysis before
execution using a defensible precision or resource rationale, not observed wins.

An independently generated synthetic study can test a scoped mechanism with
known truth. Include null/no-interaction and adverse-to-the-extension settings,
freeze mechanisms and parameter ranges before trials, and distinguish independent
trial seeds from independent scientific design. Such evidence does not establish
real-population utility or realistic corruption frequencies.

## Publication decision

A credible methods claim needs a precise limitation, a corresponding change,
added value over simpler alternatives, and evidence suited to the claim. A formal
empirical-admissibility property follows from the Pareto rule itself; it is not a
new population guarantee. Prior work already covers probability-sensitive SAFE,
integrated-score uncertainty, and Pareto risk-control methods. Complete the
relevant full-methods prior-art review before claiming novelty or choosing a
formal risk-control comparator.

The exploratory results should determine the honest scope of the manuscript,
not be optimized until a preferred superiority narrative appears.
