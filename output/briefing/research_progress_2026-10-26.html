02 / How the work is organized

One dataset.
Clearly separated roles.

The data contain 30,000 Taiwanese credit-card records. The outcome is whether a client defaults the next month: 0 = nondefault, 1 = default. The new pilot reuses the saved fitting and selection assignments.

Selection: where the new results come from

These records were not used to fit the four pilot models. We use them for development diagnostics and SAFE assessment. They are development evidence, not independent confirmation.

Keep matching records togetherRecords with identical values across all 22 original predictors stay in the same partition. No such group crosses fitting, selection or holdout.
Learn preparation from fitting dataNumeric scaling and category encoding are fitted separately for each new candidate, using fitting records only. Fixed cleaning rules do not learn from selection data.
Keep metadata out of the modelID, sex, observed outcome and duplicate-group identifiers are metadata. The models receive only the 22 predictors.
What are the inputs, and why are there 85 transformed columns?

The 22 original inputs describe credit limit, age, education, marital status, repayment status, bill amounts and previous payments. Eight categorical predictors are encoded into indicators. Together with 14 numeric inputs, this produces 85 transformed columns. The mapping back to all 22 original predictors is saved for every candidate.

The fixed historical cleaning maps EDUCATION 0/5/6 to 4 and MARRIAGE 0 to 3. Other repayment codes remain distinct. All records are retained. Decisions use a strict default probability above 0.50; exactly 0.50 is class 0. That rule is unchanged and is not needed for the SAFE rank-based curves.

Earlier versus new training: the original models were fitted on all 24,004 training records. The four new pilot candidates were fitted only on the 19,203 fitting records. Earlier holdout results and new selection results therefore describe different fits and evaluation roles.

The four fixed configurations

Logistic Regression: L2 regularization, C=1, lbfgs, up to 2,000 iterations.
Decision Tree: maximum depth 5; minimum 20 records per leaf.
Random Forest: 300 trees; minimum 5 records per leaf; sqrt feature sampling; one worker.
Gradient Boosting: 100 trees; learning rate .1; depth 3.

What stayed the same

All random seeds are 42. Each candidate independently learns numeric scaling and one-hot category encoding from fitting data. No feature selection, reweighting, calibration, oversampling or hyperparameter search was added.

All four fitted without warnings. Logistic Regression converged in 71 iterations. There were no missing/nonfinite inputs or unseen selection categories. Fitting time includes preprocessing and estimator fitting.

What does the apparent difference mean?

Gradient Boosting has the highest observed ROC-AUC and average precision, and the lowest Brier and LogLoss, among these four selection-set fits. Logistic Regression has the highest integrated SAFE score under our declared specification.

Those results answer different questions. We have not accepted any of these predictive diagnostics as the final judge of model-selection quality.

A fixed pool of candidate models

These are four specific, untuned configurations. A result for this Decision Tree or Random Forest is not a verdict on every possible version of that algorithm.

Both selection methods will receive this same frozen candidate pool. No model has been refitted on fitting plus selection records.

04 / What SAFE actually does

Three curves.
One declared selection rule.

Integrated SAFE 2026 combines accuracy, explainability preservation and robustness. We implemented its published equations with explicit Taiwan choices where the paper leaves operational details open.

Step 0 / 22

How the score is combined

We sample 23 points on each curve, including both endpoints. At each combination of an accuracy point, a preservation point and a robustness point, we calculate a mean. We then average all 12,167 combinations.

The prespecified primary version uses the arithmetic mean under percentile swapping. Geometric and quadratic means are sensitivity checks. All three select Logistic Regression here, with no ties.

SAFE .652 is a constructed index.
It does not mean 65.2% predictive accuracy.

Why Logistic Regression is selected here

Under the arithmetic rule, Logistic Regression’s higher average preservation and robustness values outweigh its slightly lower average accuracy-curve value.

Colored lengths are each component’s sampled mean divided by three. This decomposition applies to arithmetic aggregation only.

Compare the three integrated scores and inspect the implementation choices
Integrated SAFE scores under percentile swapping

What comes from the paper

The common Lorenz/concordance ratio, cumulative RGA contribution accounting, reversed RGE, swapping severity from 0 to .5 normalized to 0–1, and cellwise arithmetic, geometric and quadratic means.

Raw curve values enter the score. Curve areas and sampled means are saved separately. We do not add fairness, scale curves by accuracy, normalize across candidates, or replace undefined values with zero.

What we explicitly chose

Full-sample additive accuracy contributions; tie-block averaging; whole-original-predictor masking at fitting-only numeric means/categorical modes; a greedy least-damaging removal search; deterministic tail pairing; and 23 endpoint-inclusive nodes.

These are approved Taiwan operationalizations, not a claim to reproduce the authors’ HMDA tables exactly. The later author-credited package was inspected without installation; its differing conventions were not silently adopted.

Unavailable alternatives: Gaussian assessment is deferred by user decision because categorical treatment is unresolved. Exact TOPSIS remains unsupported because its published setup is insufficiently specified.

Explore the greedy removal sequence

At each step we test every remaining predictor and replace the one that best preserves the full model’s ranking. This is a fresh greedy decision at every step. The primary model is never retrained.

253 alternatives per model, 1,012 altogether. Feature names are original predictor groups, so categorical indicators are handled together. Score ties within 1e-12 use the saved predictor order.

Original LR / GBM study · historical 5,996-record holdout

Better ranking still leaves many defaults undetected.

Gradient Boosting ranks outcomes better and has lower probability errors on this sample. Both models detect about one in three defaults at the chosen threshold. That alone does not establish an overall recommendation.

  • Group differences: female-minus-male recall gaps were +2.32 percentage points for LR and +3.35 for GBM. Their intervals include zero. This is not proof of fairness or equivalence.
  • Explanations: repayment status PAY_0 and credit limit LIMIT_BAL dominate SHAP contributions. The top three account for 54.2% of total importance for LR and 40.9% for GBM. Concentration is not human explanation quality.
  • Synthetic sensitivity: the declared supplementary scenario, which corrupted about 10% of records, produced decision changes of 0.79% for LR and 1.30% for GBM. Native SAFE swapping of the 5% tails in each predictor produced 11.44% and 11.12%. These are different exposures and severities, so they are not equivalent tests.

The procedure stopped when adding the fourth predictor, PAY_2, did not improve validation ranking. Its selected model’s holdout ROC-AUC was .7512, compared with .7781 for the full model.

This uses 19 fewer predictors, with about .027 lower AUC. Whether the trade-off is practically worthwhile needs an explicit criterion.

The article’s logistic model is unavailable. Some encoded inputs contained redundant information, so its specified unpenalized coefficient estimates could not be uniquely determined. We preserved that limitation; the regularized pilot LR is a separate model.
Important differences from the current pilot

The article branch selected inputs on fitting/validation data and refitted its GBMs on all 24,004 training records before holdout assessment. It did not produce the integrated SAFE 2026 score. The current pilot instead keeps all four models frozen after fitting on 19,203 records and assesses them only on the selection set.

The selected-minus-full AUC difference was −.02695, with a conditional paired 95% interval [−.03485, −.01895]. This interval conditions on fixed fits and the selected subset; it does not include refitting or selection uncertainty.

06 / What we can conclude

A verified selection.
An open comparison.

We have completed a reproducible SAFE assessment of a common set of candidates. We have not yet shown that SAFE, or our developing approach, makes the better decision.

Established so far

The original assessments and the separate article adaptation are complete. Four new, fixed candidates are fitted and verified. The declared arithmetic SAFE swapping rule selects Logistic Regression on the selection set; its geometric and quadratic sensitivity versions agree.

These results are conditional on this candidate pool, preprocessing, partitions and operational choices. A high SAFE score does not establish legal compliance, real-world robustness, human explanation quality or independent predictive superiority.

The holdout has already informed the project. Taiwan is exploratory development evidence and cannot be relabeled untouched confirmation data.

Metric glossary

ROC-AUC: how well scores rank a randomly chosen default above a nondefault; ties receive half credit. Higher is better.

Average precision (AP): a summary of precision across recall levels, calculated with sklearn’s definition. Higher is better.

Brier / LogLoss: errors in predicted probabilities. Lower is better. They measure different aspects of probability quality.

Recall: the proportion of observed defaults flagged by the model at the stated threshold.

RGA: rank graduation accuracy. The scalar equals ROC-AUC for binary outcomes with our tie treatment; its cumulative curve accounts for ranked area contributions.

RGR: preservation of the original model’s output ordering under changed inputs, measured with the common concordance ratio.

Original RGE: one minus preservation after input removal. The aggregate uses the reversed preservation curve, so the directions are explicitly distinguished.

Conditional uncertainty: uncertainty holding fitted models and selected inputs fixed; it does not include every source of research uncertainty.
