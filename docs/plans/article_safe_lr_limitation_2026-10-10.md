# The article LR limitation and what it means for comparison

10 October 2026. Andrei authorized finishing the existing article specification, explicitly without pooling repayment categories. This note records the discussion; it does not introduce a new encoding rule or experiment.

Our original project uses regularized logistic regression. The article branch uses an unpenalized binomial GLM so that its ordinary nested likelihood comparisons have the intended statistical interpretation. Article sections 2.2 and 3.1 describe likelihood-based selection and maximum-likelihood estimation. They do not explicitly forbid regularization. Unpenalized estimation is our declared interpretation of that procedure, not a quoted blanket ban. Regularized LR remains a reasonable predictive estimator, but its estimates cannot simply be substituted into the same ordinary likelihood and coefficient tests.

The six repayment columns are monthly status snapshots, not binary source variables. Positive codes indicate months of delay. The timeline is PAY_6 April, PAY_5 May, PAY_4 June, PAY_3 July, PAY_2 August and PAY_0 September 2005. We have not established a deterministic rule for how partial payments change the next month's code. The cited UCI description does not define -2 and 0; the original implementation preserves them as distinct categories and does not treat them as numbers of months. Semantic robustness leaves these special states unchanged.

Both our original pipelines and the article specification encode repayment statuses categorically. The article does not demonstrably mandate that encoding. In the article LR, reference coding creates indicators such as "PAY_4 equals 8." It does not replace every repayment column by one binary overdue flag. Every record still has a repayment status for every month.

In fitting data, the full article design has 78 columns but rank 74; in all training data it has rank 76. PAY_4=8, PAY_5=8 and PAY_6=8 identify exactly the same single training record. Those particular indicators are redundant; the original monthly columns contain many other values and are not identical. Other dependencies also exist. Individual ordinary coefficients are not uniquely identifiable in this design. Some rare indicators also contain only one outcome class, so merely removing aliases would not guarantee a finite unpenalized estimate. Evidence is saved in the [training diagnosis](../../article_safe/runs/article_v1_2026-10-10/diagnostics/lr_design_identifiability.json) and [rare-category support](../../article_safe/runs/article_v1_2026-10-10/diagnostics/rare_repayment_indicator_support.csv).

Regularization permits the original predictive LR to fit despite redundancy, but does not create data evidence separating those rare categories' individual effects. The article roles and dependent tests are therefore unavailable under the approved v1 full-rank contract. The backward procedure is implemented and passed an identifiable synthetic fixture; no successful credit-data LR reduction is claimed. This limitation is a property of our estimator/design/data combination, not proof that LR predicts poorly or that the article's framework is inferior.

Pooling adjacent rare positive statuses, for example a six-plus-month category, was proposed and explicitly deferred. Such a category would impose a common effect for the merged levels and could change coefficients, statistical tests, feature selection, predictions, explanations and group metrics. No cutoff has been agreed, no code has been pooled, and neither the original models nor the article models have been adapted in response to the discussion.

For the later framework comparison, use shared candidate models and preprocessing where feasible. If estimator or encoding differences are necessary, identify and assess them separately before attributing improvements to the framework. The original regularized LR versus article unpenalized LR is already an estimator distinction. Unavailable article outputs cannot be scored as worse performance. The current GBM reduction result supplies one observable trade-off; demonstrating superiority still requires a defined criterion and evidence. Andrei will review the completed results before deciding whether to repair the LR branch or proceed with the usable comparison scope.
