5. Discussion
The prioritization of robustness, accuracy, fairness, and explainability in AI systems is
highlycontext-dependentandshouldbecarefullyconsideredforeachapplication.Inhigh-
stakesdecision-makingscenarios,suchascreditriskassessmentforlargecorporateloans,
explainabilitymighttakeprecedence.Here,theabilitytoprovideclear,understandablerea-
sonsforamodel’spredictionsiscrucialforregulatorycomplianceandbuildingtrustwith
stakeholders.Conversely,inhigh-frequencytradingapplications,accuracyandrobustness
mightbeprioritized.
The rapid pace of transactions and the potential for significant financial losses make
it essential that models perform consistently and accurately under varying market con-
ditions. However, this doesn’t negate the importance of explainability in these contexts,
particularlyforregulatoryoversightandriskmanagement.
For consumer-facing applications like personal loan approvals or insurance pricing,
fairness often becomes a top priority. Ensuring that models don’t discriminate based on
protected characteristics is not only an ethical imperative but often a legal requirement.
However, this must be balanced with accuracy to ensure the financial viability of these
products.

In contrast, Babaei et al.’s RGB method offers a more streamlined and computation-

TheRGBapproachisgroundedintheuseofconcordancecurves,whicharesimplerand
easier to interpret, making it accessible to a broader audience. The internal consistency

scale applications or real-time monitoring. However, the simplicity of the RGB method

et al. [14] is excellent for general assessments, on the other hand it may overlook more
subtledistributionalchanges,particularlyincomplexfinancialscenarioswheretheshifts

ally, the RGB approach may be less effective in handling highly irregular or complex
distributions, where the geometric precision of the Wasserstein distance becomes more
valuable.
These considerations suggest that while our Wasserstein distance-based approach is
morerobustandadaptableincomplex,high-riskenvironments,theRGBmethodoffersa
