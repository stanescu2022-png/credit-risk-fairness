We propose a decomposition of the RGA metric into a vector of
partial RGA values, each corresponding to a segment of the ranked
predictions. This approach goes beyond the use of a single summary
score and provides a more detailed view of how different parts of the
data contribute to the overall accuracy of the model.
This decomposition is particularly important in the context of the
unified compliance score, where we simulate cyber-attacks that selec-
tively damage or degrade parts of the data. In such scenarios, relying
solely on the final RGA score is insufficient, as it may hide local weak-
nesses or failure points. By breaking the RGA into partial contributions,
we can assess how specific segments of the model’s ranking respond to
such disruptions. This allows for a more robust evaluation of the model
accuracy under adverse conditions.

ranked values to the lowest-ranked values, following a reversed con-
